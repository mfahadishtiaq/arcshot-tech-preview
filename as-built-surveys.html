What it is

An as-built survey records a building as it stands today. Every room is measured on site and drawn up, so the people working on it start from drawings that match the building.

You choose the level of detail, from a simple floor plan to a detailed layout with sections and elevations. For architects, designers, contractors and property managers.

Three levels of detail

LevelsLevels

01

Schematic floor plan

Rooms named and measured, with square footage for each. The plan a listing needs.

02

As-built layout

Every wall, door and ceiling height, with finishes noted. The drawing an architect works from.

03

Sections and elevations

The building cut through and seen from each side, added when the job calls for a full set.
